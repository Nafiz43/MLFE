Does the forecast rank industries by what they earn?

The forecaster's published direction accuracy is measured on its training target — the next four weeks' average against the last four weeks' average — and half of that target is already known at the anchor. This table scores the same forecasts against the buy-and-hold return the Planner quotes, with the market level removed, which is the only thing the allocator's industry ranking can act on. Anchors overlap almost completely at the longer horizons, so the p-value is Newey–West (HAC) rather than i.i.d., and the independent-period count is shown next to it: a strong-looking result off five independent periods is a hint, not a finding.
