How to use MarketLattice

Ask about SEC 8-K filings, stock prices & world events — in plain English.

💬 Just ask

Type your question in the box at the bottom and press Enter. You don't need to know any database or query language — the assistant works out the data, shows the exact query it ran, and replies in plain words.

🗂️ What you can explore

  • 📄 SEC 8-K filings — companies, event types (earnings, leadership changes, bankruptcies…), and filing dates.
  • 💹 Stock prices — how a company's share price moved around its filing date.
  • 🌍 World events — protests, conflict, and policy moves by country, with cross-source corroboration.
  • 📈 Correlations — which 8-K event types line up most with stock-price reactions.

📊 Turn an answer into a chart

Add “plot”, “graph”, or “chart” to your question and the answer is drawn as a bar, line, or pie chart — then download it as PNG or SVG from the chart's toolbar.

💡 Handy tips

  • Click an example chip just above the input box to start instantly.
  • Click any query panel in an answer to expand or collapse the database query and its results.
  • Your chats are saved in the sidebar (toggle with ☰) — on this device only.
  • Save a whole conversation, charts included, with ⤓ Export PDF.
  • Switch light / dark mode with the ☀️ button.
